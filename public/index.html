About sensor firmware

Mi Flora sensors on firmware 3.x broadcast their readings, so the Pi can just listen. Older 2.x firmware has to be connected to for every reading, which is less reliable and uses more battery. Firmware 3.2.1 and newer no longer broadcast battery level, so the Pi connects about once a day to read it.
